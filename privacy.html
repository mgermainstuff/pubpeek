PubPeek privacy policy

PubPeek opens and converts your Publisher files on your own computer. Your files are never uploaded, and the app has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

  • Files: read and converted locally in the app. They do not leave your device.
  • Network: the app downloads its own program files so it can work offline. It sends nothing about you or your documents.
  • Purchases: handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy terms. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Local storage: one setting that remembers the app was installed from the Store.

Questions: mgermainproducts@gmail.com
